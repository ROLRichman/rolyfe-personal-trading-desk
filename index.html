OPTIONS CONTRACT STUDY · LONG CONTRACT ONLY

Use the underlying chart levels above to study the call / put trigger. The option premium fields below are separate manual notes; this desk does not create historical option candles or convert the underlying move into an option price.

For Long plans, the stop belongs below entry and targets rise from T1 to T3. For Short plans, the stop belongs above entry and targets step downward. Entry defaults to the selected candle close when left blank. Define 1R as the distance from entry to stop; each target's R multiple is calculated from that risk distance.

PLAN VISUAL — NOT MARKET DATA. Hollow or solid future bars are schematic study candles derived from your entry and first target, not provider candles, price forecasts or backtest results. Advancing replay beyond the setup replaces them with actual historical bars. The bracket remains so you can review what price later did. In options mode, the chart displays the underlying trigger levels only; premium values remain separate notes, not option-chain history.

How to use RO’Lyfe Future Trading · step-by-step

1 · Pick the market and timeframe. Load a symbol and provider-sourced history. Start with a timeframe you understand; use the same timeframe while reviewing a setup.

2 · Find a decision candle. Drag the Historical Replay slider to any earlier candle. Pause there. Study the candle close, EMA 20 / 50 / 200, VWAP, RSI, MACD, Stochastic, HMA 32 and the RL Pullback checklist where available.

3 · Build the bracket. Click Future Bars. Choose Buy / Long or Sell / Short, select a scenario, set an entry (blank uses that candle’s close), define the invalidation stop and add up to three targets. The desk calculates each target’s R multiple from the entry-to-stop distance.

4 · Write the reason, not just the price. Note what you saw, what had to happen to confirm the entry, and what would make you skip it. Save the plan or choose Save & Ask Atlas for a step-by-step lesson.

5 · Reveal what actually happened. Advance replay one bar at a time. The illustrative future bars disappear as actual historical candles arrive. The notebook reports the first stop/target range reached; if stop and target fall inside the same candle, it marks the sequence ambiguous and asks you to inspect a lower timeframe.

6 · Translate the setup to options later. Options mode keeps the underlying chart trigger separate from manually-entered contract premium notes. A call/put premium is affected by implied volatility, time decay, spread and contract terms; this desk does not infer option premium from the underlying chart. Verify the historical contract chain and premium before judging an option result.

What is 1R? One R is the planned risk from entry to stop. For example, a $2.00 entry-to-stop distance means each $2.00 of favorable movement is 1R per share/unit; T1 at $4.00 favorable distance is 2R. A setup can be skipped—practice does not require placing trades.

AI role: Atlas is the teacher and review partner. It explains the selected replay candle, RL confluence, confirmation, invalidation, skip conditions and R-multiples from the data available at that replay point. It does not predict future bars, certify a winning setup or place orders.
